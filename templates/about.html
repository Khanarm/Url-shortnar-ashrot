About URL Shortener

URL Shortener is a fast, secure and reliable platform that helps you convert long URLs into short, easy-to-share links.

Our goal is to provide a simple and powerful URL shortening service with features like custom aliases, click analytics, search, dashboard management, and more.

We continuously improve our platform to deliver a better experience for everyone.
